# Example: AI Club Week 2

A real deck made by a person for a high school AI Club meeting (about 45 minutes, beginners). It's the model this repo was built from. Below is what step 1 of the prompt asks Claude to do: study the deck's habits before building anything.

## The habits (what Claude should notice)

1. **Uneven amounts of text.** The news slides are dense, about 100 to 200 words in full sentences, because they tell a story. The video, quote and ending slides have 10 to 50 words.
2. **Titles are 2 to 5 words in Title Case.** Section labels use a period: "In the News. What Was the Test?"
3. **New words get defined on the spot,** in a "Words to know" box on the right side of the slide.
4. **About half the content slides end with a line in the accent color** that adds a comparison, a quote or a reminder.
5. **The layout changes with the content:** bullets, a timeline list, big numbers, a single quote, two columns, a timer.
6. **The only images are video thumbnails.** No icons, emoji or stock art.
7. **Content sits at the top.** The bottom of many slides is just empty.
8. **It opens with a numbered agenda and ends with one question.**

## What it shows

The deck isn't fancy. It's one template used consistently, plain sentences, and layouts picked for what each slide needs to say. That's the target.
